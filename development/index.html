A public record of the work.

Sagewire.io is the home for SageWire development reporting. Reports will explain what changed, show the evidence available, and make the limits clear.

  1. The problem: what we set out to solve and why it matters.
  2. The change: what was built or adjusted, with the relevant version or build.
  3. The test: the procedure, environment, and number of attempts where available.
  4. The finding: what we observed, supported by demonstrations, records, or reproducible examples.
  5. The limit: what failed, remains untested, or needs further work.

Plans, implemented changes, automated checks, and observations from actual use will be identified separately. A passing check will be reported with its scope; it will not stand in for an unperformed field or gameplay test.
