Built for the field, not the desk.

SageWire captures the event where it happens, then turns speech, measurements, identifiers, location, and context into structured records that can be used, searched, shared, audited, and acted on.

The problem

Most software waits until later.

Someone does the work. Then somebody has to remember what happened, type it into a system, and reconstruct the details after the fact. SageWire is built around a different idea: capture the event when the event happens.

Less reconstruction.

Capture voice, identifiers, measurements, location, weather, photos, and timing while the event is still happening.

Less re-entry.

Use equipment and workflows already present in the field instead of creating another form somebody has to remember to complete later.

Better records.

Turn field activity into structured artifacts that can be searched, shared, audited, and carried forward.

Useful automation.

Once the event is digital, SageWire can route it, enrich it, notify someone, update another system, or trigger the next action.

Real-world activity in. Usable records out.

How SageWire works

One platform. Specialized services.

Applications stay focused on the job in front of the user. SageWire handles reusable infrastructure underneath: speech, location, weather, onboarding, memory, RFID, weighing, domain assistants, and orchestration.

01 · CAPTURE

The event happens.

A person speaks, a tag is read, a weight is measured, or a field action is recorded.

02 · ENRICH

SageWire adds context.

Location, weather, timestamps, AI processing, domain knowledge, and other service capabilities are attached where useful.

03 · RECORD

The event becomes durable.

The result can be stored, searched, shared, audited, carried forward, or used to trigger the next action.
